How to export your Instagram following

Bring your crafting connections with you. Download a copy of the accounts you follow on Instagram, then use CraftSky to find people who have joined here.

This guide is for importing your following list. It does not import your Instagram posts or the people who follow you.

1. Request your Instagram export

  1. In Instagram, open your profile and tap the menu in the top corner. Find Accounts Center in settings.
  2. Go to Your information and permissions, then Export your information.
  3. Choose Create export, select the Instagram account you want to use, and choose Export to device.
  4. Open the information selection (sometimes called Customize information or Some of your information). Select Followers and following under Connections. You can leave the other categories out.
  5. Set the format to JSON and the date range to All time, then start the export.

Instagram changes its menus from time to time, so the labels may look slightly different. Look for the export or download controls in Accounts Center.

2. Download the ZIP

Instagram will notify you when the export is ready. Return to the export screen in Accounts Center and save the ZIP to your device.

If Instagram asks you to confirm your password, do that in Instagram. CraftSky does not need your Instagram password.

3. Import it into CraftSky

  1. Open Find people from Instagram in CraftSky's settings.
  2. Complete the Instagram account verification shown in the app if you have not already done so.
  3. Under Import accounts you follow, choose Instagram export.
  4. Tap Select Instagram export and pick the ZIP you downloaded. You do not need to unzip it first.

If you already extracted the archive, you can select following.json, usually inside connections/followers_and_following. Choose the following file rather than followers_1.json.

CraftSky reads the file on your device and sends only the usernames of accounts you follow. If you downloaded all your Instagram information, the other contents of the archive stay on your device.

Matching depends on people joining CraftSky, verifying their Instagram account, and allowing discovery. An import may not find everyone straight away. You can review the suggestions and choose whom to follow.

If the import does not work

  • HTML instead of JSON? Request another export with JSON selected.
  • Missing accounts? Check that you selected All time and exported the correct Instagram account.
  • The file is too large? Request an export containing only Followers and following.
  • No export yet? Check Accounts Center again later for its status.

You can also choose Enter handles in CraftSky and paste the Instagram usernames you want to import, one per line.
