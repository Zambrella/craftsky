CraftSky Community Guidelines

Version 1.0. Effective date: 10 November 2026.

CraftSky is a social network for textile and fibre crafters. We want it to be a place where people can share projects, ask questions, learn, and celebrate each other's work without being harassed, deceived, or exploited.

These Guidelines explain what is allowed on CraftSky. They apply alongside our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

The Short Version

  • Be kind and respect other people.
  • Do not post illegal content, threats, hate, harassment, exploitation, scams, spam, or sexually explicit content.
  • Do not share another person's private or intimate material without permission.
  • Be honest about who you are and disclose commercial relationships clearly.
  • Keep CraftSky mainly about textile and fibre crafts.
  • Do not post creative work materially generated by generative AI.
  • Post only content you have the right to share.
  • Report serious concerns rather than redistributing harmful material.

Safety

Content harmful to children

CraftSky does not allow:

  • pornography;
  • content encouraging, promoting or giving instructions for suicide, deliberate self-injury or eating-disorder behaviour;
  • bullying, abusive content targeting protected characteristics, or content inciting hatred against protected groups;
  • encouragement, promotion or instructions for serious violence against a person;
  • depictions of real or realistic serious violence against people, or graphic depictions of their real or realistic serious injuries;
  • depictions of real or realistic serious violence against animals, graphic depictions of their real or realistic serious injuries, or realistic depictions of serious violence or graphic serious injury involving fictional creatures;
  • challenges or stunts highly likely to cause serious injury, or encouragement to ingest, inhale, inject or otherwise self-administer harmful substances or harmful quantities of a substance;
  • content shaming or stigmatizing body types or physical features; or
  • content promoting depression, hopelessness or despair.

These restrictions apply to everyone using child-accessible parts of CraftSky, including adults. Educational, medical, artistic or historical context matters when deciding what content actually shows or promotes. It is not a blanket exception where content still meets a harmful-content definition above.

Ordinary garment and fit discussion, inclusive size information, safe craft instruction, fibre sourcing and welfare discussion, legitimate non-graphic health or educational material, and supportive accounts of grief, illness, recovery or seeking help remain welcome when they do not meet those definitions. Ordinary unfinished craft objects, fictional characters, manageable everyday tool risks and neutral body or size facts are not automatically harmful content.

Violence, threats, and criminal activity

Do not threaten, praise, encourage, or help carry out violence, terrorism, organized crime, exploitation, trafficking, fraud, or other criminal activity. Do not use CraftSky to sell or facilitate illegal drugs, weapons, or criminal property.

Journalism, counterspeech, victim testimony, academic or documentary discussion, news, history, fiction, prevention, recovery, and safety information may be allowed when they do not promote harm, have appropriate context and do not otherwise meet the harmful-content definitions above.

Child safety

Do not sexualize, groom, exploit, or abuse a child. Never post, request, link to, store, or share child sexual abuse material. Do not try to move a child into unsafe contact on another service.

If you encounter suspected child sexual exploitation or abuse material, do not download, copy, quote-post, or send it to other users. Use the reporting route and provide the location of the content without redistributing it.

Sexual and intimate content

CraftSky is not an adult-content service. Do not post or link to pornography, sexually explicit content, explicit nudity, fetish content, cyberflashing, or sexual exploitation.

Never share or threaten to share a real or altered intimate image of another person without their consent.

Craft projects and links involving sexual health, breastfeeding, swimwear, underwear, lingerie, life drawing, medical or post-surgery garments, gender- affirming garments, abuse prevention, support resources, or historical material may be allowed when the context is legitimate, not sexually exploitative and does not otherwise meet a harmful-content definition above.

Suicide, self-injury and eating disorders

Do not encourage, promote, assist or give instructions for suicide, deliberate self-injury or eating-disorder behaviour. Do not target someone with content promoting those harms. This rule is not limited to criminal conduct or injury classified as serious. Supportive discussion of recovery, personal experience, and seeking help is welcome when it does not provide harmful instruction or encouragement.

Depression, hopelessness and despair

Do not promote depression, hopelessness or despair, including by encouraging someone to give up seeking help. Sharing that you feel depressed, grieving or overwhelmed is different from promoting those harms. Honest accounts of illness, difficult feelings, recovery and seeking support remain welcome.

Dangerous challenges and harmful substances

Do not encourage, promote or give instructions for challenges or stunts highly likely to seriously injure someone. Do not encourage people to ingest, inhale, inject or otherwise self-administer a harmful substance or a harmful quantity of one. Ordinary safe use of craft tools, dyes and other materials, proportionate safety warnings and legitimate non-harmful instruction remain welcome.

Graphic or distressing material

Do not post material that meets the serious-violence or graphic-serious-injury prohibitions above, including to shock or intimidate. Educational, medical, historical, protest or recovery context does not automatically exempt it. Some less graphic or distressing craft-related material may be allowed where it has legitimate context and does not meet a harmful-content definition.

CraftSky does not currently provide a poster-controlled sensitive-content label. Use a non-graphic image or a clear text explanation where it communicates the craft point safely. A content warning does not make otherwise prohibited material acceptable. Moderators assess the actual content and context before applying proportionate action.

Respect Other People

Harassment and unwanted contact

Do not bully, intimidate, stalk, threaten, repeatedly target, or encourage a dogpile against another person. Do not create an account whose purpose is to harass someone.

Respect blocks. Deliberately continuing unwanted contact through another account, another person, mentions, quotes, or another route may be treated as block evasion. A CraftSky block restricts contact and visibility in compliant CraftSky clients; it cannot guarantee isolation across the whole AT Protocol network.

Hate

Do not attack, dehumanize, threaten, or promote hatred or discrimination against people based on characteristics such as race, ethnicity, nationality, disability, religion, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity, or age.

Privacy and intimate information

Do not share another person's address, phone number, private email, financial details, precise location, identity documents, medical information, private communications, or other sensitive identifying information without permission.

Bodies and fit feedback

Do not degrade, sexualize, shame or stigmatize bodies or physical features, whether or not a particular person is targeted. Keep fit feedback respectful, focused on the garment, and responsive to what the poster asked for.

Authenticity and Trust

Spam, manipulation, scams, and impersonation

Do not flood CraftSky with duplicated, automated, deceptive, or irrelevant content. Do not use misleading tags, engagement manipulation, unsolicited mass mentions, phishing, counterfeit sales, fake shops, or financial scams.

Do not pretend to be another person, shop, designer, or organization. Parody and fan accounts must be clearly identified and must follow the rest of these rules.

Commercial transparency

Disclose sponsorship, payment, gifts, affiliate relationships, discounts, or any other material benefit connected with a post. Use CraftSky's disclosure controls where available and use wording that makes the commercial relationship obvious. If no control is available, place a clear label such as Ad, Sponsored, Affiliate link, or Gifted product where people will see it before engaging.

Businesses are welcome. Relevant promotion is allowed when it is not deceptive, duplicative, or spammy. Business accounts do not receive preferential distribution. A paid subscription does not provide additional reach.

Reviews and factual claims

Honest criticism of patterns, products, shops, creators, and conduct is allowed. Focus on the work, product, service, or conduct rather than personal abuse. Do not make materially false claims about an identifiable person or business.

Do not knowingly give false or dangerously misleading health, safety, legal, standards, chemical, electrical, or baby-product advice. CraftSky may add a warning, reduce visibility, or remove content according to the seriousness and confidence of the risk.

Craft Content

Keep it relevant

Most public posts should relate to textile and fibre projects, techniques, materials, tools, tutorials, inspiration, businesses, or the wider craft community. Occasional off-topic posts are welcome, but accounts used mainly for unrelated content may be moderated.

Political and social topics are welcome when they are meaningfully connected to craft, creative work, labour, identity, accessibility, the environment, or the craft community. General party campaigning, electioneering, and unrelated streams of political commentary are off-topic.

Generative AI

Creative content materially generated by generative AI is not currently allowed. This includes generated images, video, audio, writing presented as creative work, and generated patterns.

Spelling and grammar tools, translation, accessibility tools, background removal, and ordinary image correction are allowed when they do not materially generate the creative work. If this policy changes, CraftSky will explain the change and any disclosure or filtering controls before it takes effect.

Cultural and traditional designs

Treat cultural traditions with respect. Give context and attribution where appropriate. Material that exploits, seriously misrepresents, or improperly uses sacred or culturally restricted designs may be removed after contextual review. Where practical, CraftSky will consider credible information from the relevant community, explain an owner-visible decision, and provide an appeal.

Copyright and trade marks

Use native reposts or links where possible. Do not copy and re-upload another creator's work without permission unless a lawful exception applies. Credit does not replace permission. Do not distribute paid patterns, magazine pages, or other copyrighted material without authorization, remove watermarks, sell counterfeit goods, or misuse trade marks.

See the Copyright and Trade Mark Policy for notice, counter-notice, and restoration procedures.

Profiles, Links, and Platform Integrity

These rules apply to handles, display names, profile images, bios, links, events, posts, comments, and other CraftSky surfaces. Linking to prohibited content may be treated in the same way as posting it directly.

Do not compromise or abuse CraftSky systems or infrastructure, evade moderation, create replacement accounts to avoid suspension, scrape contrary to law or applicable terms, or manipulate feeds, search, or engagement.

How Enforcement Works

A report is an allegation, not a finding. CraftSky reviews available context and records a decision before applying a moderation consequence. Depending on the violation, severity, intent, context, history, and risk, CraftSky may:

  • give the account owner a formal warning;
  • place a warning on content that remains visible;
  • hide or take content out of CraftSky surfaces;
  • issue a strike;
  • suspend participation, which restricts specified CraftSky functions, immediately for a severe violation or after three active strikes.

Other illegal content can lead to a strike or, for a severe violation, immediate suspension after a supported human review. We record the factual and legal basis and provide the account owner with a safe explanation. We use a specific policy category where it fits. An unspecified “Other” report alone does not justify a strike or immediate suspension.

Severe fraud and deliberate encouragement or assistance of serious self-harm can justify immediate suspension. Ordinary transaction disputes, distress, recovery and help-seeking are not grounds by themselves. Reports, report counts and automated signals are not findings of wrongdoing.

Strikes remain active for 12 calendar months unless reversed. An account is suspended while at least three strikes remain active. A severe suspension remains in place until CraftSky restores the account or reverses the decision. Enforcement remains in place while an appeal is reviewed. Suspension affects participation through CraftSky; it does not deactivate a DID or PDS account or remove the person from the wider AT Protocol network.

Suspended members retain read access and can review moderation history, appeal, manage private settings and subscriptions, make privacy requests, remove their own content, sign out, and delete their CraftSky account. They cannot post or interact publicly while suspended.

CraftSky visibility actions do not delete a user's source record from their PDS. Independent AT Protocol services may continue to display public records under their own rules. CraftSky may preserve limited evidence where law requires it.

Every owner-visible violation decision can be appealed using the case reference shown in moderation history. No-action outcomes and private report details are not shown to the reported account.

Reporting

Use the report action on a post, profile, account, or business event where available. People who cannot sign in, affected non-users, and representatives can email moderation@craftsky.social. Pseudonymous reports are accepted where possible, although some legal routes require identity or contact information. Do not repost harmful material merely to draw attention to it.

See Reporting, Complaints, and Appeals.

Eligibility

You must be at least 16 years old to use CraftSky. CraftSky does not currently verify age. People who do not meet the age requirement must not create or use a CraftSky membership. CraftSky nevertheless treats the service as likely to be accessed by children and applies the safeguards required by law.
