Safety and support

You deserve to feel safe while sharing your craft. This guide is available without signing in, for young people, parents and carers.

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services. CraftSky is not an emergency service.

For young people

You can enjoy CraftSky without sharing your address, school, phone number or private information. You do not have to reply to someone who makes you uncomfortable. Report the concern, block or mute an account, or talk to someone you trust. Do not copy or forward harmful images to make a report.

Ways to take control

  1. !

    Report

    Open the post, profile or event menu and choose Report. Tell us where the concern is; do not forward harmful images. CraftSky reviews reports before deciding what action to take.

  2. ×

    Block

    Open the person's profile menu and choose Block to restrict contact and visibility in CraftSky. Blocks cannot guarantee isolation across independent AT Protocol services.

  3. −

    Mute

    Open the person's profile menu and choose Mute to reduce what you see from them in CraftSky. Muting is private, but does not stop them contacting you.

Bluesky messages

CraftSky messages use Bluesky's shared inbox. Use the message or conversation report action for concerns there; those reports go to Bluesky, not CraftSky's moderation queue. Incoming-contact settings are shared with Bluesky. Reading a request does not accept it. Consider your contact settings, and do not feel pressured to continue a conversation or move elsewhere.

For parents and carers

CraftSky's minimum age is 16, and age is self-declared rather than verified. Public posts can be copied by independent AT Protocol services. Talk together about privacy, unwanted contact, respectful body and fit discussion, and where to get help. Use the reporting, blocking and muting steps above, and the separate Bluesky route for messages.

We do not routinely share your report text or identity with the reported person. A safe moderation explanation may be given, and disclosure can occur where authorized, needed to protect someone, or otherwise permitted or required by law. No service can promise absolute confidentiality. These resources are independent of CraftSky; listing them does not imply endorsement or guaranteed availability.
