# Approved harmful-content boundary and policy alignment

## Outcome and decisions

This package records the owner-approved harmful-content boundary and local alignment of draft policies and operator guidance. The approved prohibition covers users of child-accessible CraftSky parts while retaining legitimate craft, health, grief, recovery and help-seeking context outside the prohibited definitions. Educational/artistic context informs assessment rather than automatically exempting material that still meets the covered definitions. Policy violations and criminal findings remain distinct.

## Evidence and limits

Proposal approval and validation are explicit, but apply only to the boundary and scoped documentation/artifact alignment. They do not approve all risk ratings, completed training, provider efficacy, whole-assessment compliance or public launch. Generated publication remains blocked and no runtime moderation change is claimed.
