## Current position

The owner already approved a public-content prohibition for pornography,
sexual exploitation and harmful suicide/self-harm/eating-disorder encouragement.
That intent is preserved. The children's review exposes remaining precision and
scope questions rather than seeking to reverse the prior decision:

- The four primary-priority categories include deliberate self-injury, not only
  self-injury meeting the criminal serious-harm threshold. Align the precise
  policy test with the children's category rather than importing a criminal test.
- Harassment/hate and body-shaming rules already cover much priority material.
  Dangerous challenges, harmful-substance encouragement and promotion of
  depression/hopelessness need clear coverage.
- The draft graphic-content exception permits some educational, medical,
  historical or protest material. Some such material could still meet a covered
  child-harm definition. An educational label or content warning alone is not
  evidence that children are protected.
- No reliable adult/child separation or age-restricted content area is evidenced.
  Publication and operational enforcement are still separate release work.

The approved route is an explicit all-user harmful-content prohibition and
swift response when identified, rather than introducing an adult-only content
area. It must still be tested and does not establish that age assurance is
unnecessary under every other condition or legal duty.

## Approved Guidelines wording

Place alongside the existing content rules and align the detailed sections:

> CraftSky does not allow content harmful to children, including:
>
> - pornography;
> - content encouraging, promoting or giving instructions for suicide,
>   deliberate self-injury or eating-disorder behaviour;
> - bullying, abusive content targeting protected characteristics, or content
>   inciting hatred against protected groups;
> - encouragement or instructions for serious violence against a person;
> - depictions of real or realistic serious violence or graphic serious injury
>   involving people or animals, including relevant realistic fictional-creature
>   depictions;
> - challenges or stunts highly likely to cause serious injury, or encouragement
>   to ingest, inhale, inject or otherwise self-administer harmful substances;
> - content shaming or stigmatizing body types or physical features; and
> - content promoting depression, hopelessness or despair.
>
> These rules apply to everyone using the child-accessible parts of CraftSky.
> Educational, medical, artistic or historical context is relevant to deciding
> what content actually shows or promotes; it is not a blanket exception where
> the content still meets a harmful-content definition above.
>
> Ordinary garment and fit discussion, inclusive size information, safe craft
> instruction, fibre sourcing and welfare discussion, legitimate non-graphic
> health or educational material, and supportive accounts of grief, illness,
> recovery or seeking help remain welcome when they do not meet those harmful-
> content definitions. Sharing that you feel depressed is different from
> promoting hopelessness or discouraging someone from seeking help. Prefer a
> non-graphic image or explanation where it communicates the craft point safely.

Exact drafting must remain consistent with the current Ofcom content guidance,
particularly context and the harmful-content thresholds. Ordinary unfinished
craft objects, fictional characters, everyday tool risks and neutral body/size
facts are not automatically harmful content. The proposed rule targets harmful
content, not a topic, identity, diagnosis or a person seeking support.

## Operational meaning

- Moderators review context and use a specific accurate policy reason, then
  restrict/remove CraftSky presentation proportionately where the rule is met.
  Harmful-to-children content is not necessarily criminal. Do not use
  `illegal_content` without its supported offence assessment or invent a severe
  reason solely to impose a stronger sanction. Generic Other can still support
  content removal but not strikes/severe suspension under current policy.
- No automatic sanction follows from a report, keyword, content proxy, age
  assumption or risk rating. Preserve safe owner explanations, appeals and
  restoration. Distress or legitimate recovery discussion does not merit a strike
  merely because it concerns suicide, self-harm, eating disorders or depression.
- Apply the intended prohibition across child-accessible CraftSky presentation,
  including public posts, comments, profile/event/link presentation and media or
  previews. Use actual control inventories and tests before claiming coverage.
- Bluesky-only DM reporting remains the owner-approved route. The policy
  decision does not create CraftSky transcript access, provider moderation power,
  a duplicate local report or a guarantee that provider messages can be removed.
  The remaining PCU takedown/age-control dependency for messaging must be resolved
  with evidence; neither declaring a ban nor excluding messaging on paper closes it.
- No moderator PDS/DID deletion, automatic private-media scanning, new age-data
  collection, adult-only feed or ranking change is authorized by this decision.
