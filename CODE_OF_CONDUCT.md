# Code of Conduct

CraftSky welcomes contributors of different backgrounds, identities, abilities, and experience levels. This applies to project issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project-managed community spaces.

Be respectful and constructive. Discuss the work, explain disagreements, and make room for people learning the tools. Ask before sharing another person's private information. Respect a contributor's decision to step away.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual harassment, personal attacks, and publishing private information without consent are not acceptable. Repeated disruption or unwanted contact is also not acceptable.

Report concerns privately to **support@craftsky.social**, currently handled by Doug Todd. Include links or context needed to understand the concern, while limiting personal information to what is necessary. Do not post a sensitive report publicly. This inbox is not an emergency service.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close or lock discussions, or restrict participation. They will consider the context and severity, explain decisions to the affected people where appropriate, and limit sharing of reports to people needed to handle them. Someone affected by a decision can reply privately with additional context and ask for reconsideration.

For service content reports and account moderation appeals, use [CraftSky's reporting process](https://craftsky.social/reporting). For software vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
