CraftSky is a social network for textile and fibre crafters. We want it to be a
place where people can share projects, ask questions, learn, and celebrate each
other's work without being harassed, deceived, or exploited.

These Guidelines explain what is allowed on CraftSky. They apply alongside our
Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

## The Short Version

- Be kind and respect other people.
- Do not post illegal content, threats, hate, harassment, exploitation, scams,
  spam, or sexually explicit content.
- Do not share another person's private or intimate material without permission.
- Be honest about who you are and disclose commercial relationships clearly.
- Keep CraftSky mainly about textile and fibre crafts.
- Do not post creative work materially generated by generative AI.
- Post only content you have the right to share.
- Report serious concerns rather than redistributing harmful material.

## Safety

### Violence, threats, and criminal activity

Do not threaten, praise, encourage, or help carry out violence, terrorism,
organized crime, exploitation, trafficking, fraud, or other criminal activity.
Do not use CraftSky to sell or facilitate illegal drugs, weapons, or criminal
property.

Journalism, counterspeech, victim testimony, academic or documentary discussion,
news, history, fiction, prevention, recovery, and safety information may be
allowed when they do not promote harm and have appropriate context.

### Child safety

Do not sexualize, groom, exploit, or abuse a child. Never post, request, link to,
store, or share child sexual abuse material. Do not try to move a child into
unsafe contact on another service.

If you encounter suspected child sexual exploitation or abuse material, do not
download, copy, quote-post, or send it to other users. Use the reporting route and
provide the location of the content without redistributing it.

### Sexual and intimate content

CraftSky is not an adult-content service. Do not post or link to pornography,
sexually explicit content, explicit nudity, fetish content, cyberflashing, or
sexual exploitation.

Never share or threaten to share a real or altered intimate image of another
person without their consent.

Craft projects and links involving sexual health, breastfeeding, swimwear,
underwear, lingerie, life drawing, medical or post-surgery garments, gender-
affirming garments, abuse prevention, support resources, or historical material
may be allowed when the context is legitimate and not sexually exploitative.

### Suicide and serious self-harm

Do not encourage, assist, instruct, or target someone with content promoting
suicide, serious self-harm, or dangerous eating-disorder behaviour. Supportive
discussion of recovery, personal experience, and seeking help is welcome when it
does not provide harmful instruction or encouragement.

### Graphic or distressing material

Do not post graphic violence, injury, abuse, or cruelty to shock or intimidate.
Some distressing craft-related material may be allowed where it has a clear
educational, historical, medical, protest, grief, or recovery context.

CraftSky does not currently provide a poster-controlled sensitive-content label.
Until that control exists, consider whether a less graphic image or a clear text
description can communicate the same point. Moderators may warn, hide, or remove
material depending on its context and risk.

## Respect Other People

### Harassment and unwanted contact

Do not bully, intimidate, stalk, threaten, repeatedly target, or encourage a
dogpile against another person. Do not create an account whose purpose is to
harass someone.

Respect blocks. Deliberately continuing unwanted contact through another account,
another person, mentions, quotes, or another route may be treated as block
evasion. A CraftSky block restricts contact and visibility in compliant CraftSky
clients; it cannot guarantee isolation across the whole AT Protocol network.

### Hate

Do not attack, dehumanize, threaten, or promote hatred or discrimination against
people based on characteristics such as race, ethnicity, nationality, disability,
religion, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity, or age.

### Privacy and intimate information

Do not share another person's address, phone number, private email, financial
details, precise location, identity documents, medical information, private
communications, or other sensitive identifying information without permission.

### Bodies and fit feedback

Do not degrade, sexualize, or shame another person's body or appearance. Keep fit
feedback respectful, focused on the garment, and responsive to what the poster
asked for.

## Authenticity and Trust

### Spam, manipulation, scams, and impersonation

Do not flood CraftSky with duplicated, automated, deceptive, or irrelevant
content. Do not use misleading tags, engagement manipulation, unsolicited mass
mentions, phishing, counterfeit sales, fake shops, or financial scams.

Do not pretend to be another person, shop, designer, or organization. Parody and
fan accounts must be clearly identified and must follow the rest of these rules.

### Commercial transparency

Disclose sponsorship, payment, gifts, affiliate relationships, discounts, or any
other material benefit connected with a post. Use CraftSky's disclosure controls
where available and use wording that makes the commercial relationship obvious.
If no control is available, place a clear label such as `Ad`, `Sponsored`,
`Affiliate link`, or `Gifted product` where people will see it before engaging.

Businesses are welcome. Relevant promotion is allowed when it is not deceptive,
duplicative, or spammy. Business accounts do not receive preferential
distribution. A paid subscription does not provide additional reach.

### Reviews and factual claims

Honest criticism of patterns, products, shops, creators, and conduct is allowed.
Focus on the work, product, service, or conduct rather than personal abuse. Do not
make materially false claims about an identifiable person or business.

Do not knowingly give false or dangerously misleading health, safety, legal,
standards, chemical, electrical, or baby-product advice. CraftSky may add a
warning, reduce visibility, or remove content according to the seriousness and
confidence of the risk.

## Craft Content

### Keep it relevant

Most public posts should relate to textile and fibre projects, techniques,
materials, tools, tutorials, inspiration, businesses, or the wider craft
community. Occasional off-topic posts are welcome, but accounts used mainly for
unrelated content may be moderated.

Political and social topics are welcome when they are meaningfully connected to
craft, creative work, labour, identity, accessibility, the environment, or the
craft community. General party campaigning, electioneering, and unrelated streams
of political commentary are off-topic.

### Generative AI

Creative content materially generated by generative AI is not currently allowed.
This includes generated images, video, audio, writing presented as creative work,
and generated patterns.

Spelling and grammar tools, translation, accessibility tools, background removal,
and ordinary image correction are allowed when they do not materially generate
the creative work. If this policy changes, CraftSky will explain the change and
any disclosure or filtering controls before it takes effect.

### Cultural and traditional designs

Treat cultural traditions with respect. Give context and attribution where
appropriate. Material that exploits, seriously misrepresents, or improperly uses
sacred or culturally restricted designs may be removed after contextual review.
Where practical, CraftSky will consider credible information from the relevant
community, explain an owner-visible decision, and provide an appeal.

### Copyright and trade marks

Use native reposts or links where possible. Do not copy and re-upload another
creator's work without permission unless a lawful exception applies. Credit does
not replace permission. Do not distribute paid patterns, magazine pages, or other
copyrighted material without authorization, remove watermarks, sell counterfeit
goods, or misuse trade marks.

See the [Copyright and Trade Mark Policy](copyright-and-trade-marks.md) for notice,
counter-notice, and restoration procedures.

## Profiles, Links, and Platform Integrity

These rules apply to handles, display names, profile images, bios, links,
events, posts, comments, and other CraftSky surfaces. Linking to prohibited
content may be treated in the same way as posting it directly.

Do not compromise or abuse CraftSky systems or infrastructure, evade
moderation, create replacement accounts to avoid suspension, scrape contrary to
law or applicable terms, or manipulate feeds, search, or engagement.

## How Enforcement Works

A report is an allegation, not a finding. CraftSky reviews available context and
records a decision before applying a moderation consequence. Depending on the
violation, severity, intent, context, history, and risk, CraftSky may:

- give the account owner a formal warning;
- place a warning on content that remains visible;
- hide or take content out of CraftSky surfaces;
- issue a strike;
- suspend participation, which restricts specified CraftSky functions,
  immediately for a severe violation or after
  three active strikes.

Strikes remain active for 12 calendar months unless reversed. An account is
suspended while at least three strikes remain active. A severe suspension remains
in place until CraftSky restores the account or reverses the decision. Enforcement
remains in place while an appeal is reviewed. Suspension affects participation
through CraftSky; it does not deactivate a DID or PDS account or remove the person
from the wider AT Protocol network.

Suspended members retain read access and can review moderation history, appeal,
manage private settings and subscriptions, make privacy requests, remove their
own content, sign out, and delete their CraftSky account. They cannot post or
interact publicly while suspended.

CraftSky visibility actions do not delete a user's source record from their PDS.
Independent AT Protocol services may continue to display public records under
their own rules. CraftSky may preserve limited evidence where law requires it.

Every owner-visible violation decision can be appealed using the case reference
shown in moderation history. No-action outcomes and private report details are
not shown to the reported account.

## Reporting

Use the report action on a post, profile, account, or business event where
available. People who cannot sign in, affected non-users, and representatives can
email `moderation@craftsky.social`. Pseudonymous reports are accepted where
possible, although some legal routes require identity or contact information. Do
not repost harmful material merely to draw attention to it.

See [Reporting, Complaints, and Appeals](reporting-complaints-and-appeals.md).

## Eligibility

You must be at least 16 years old to use CraftSky. CraftSky does not currently
verify age. People who do not meet the age requirement must not create or use a
CraftSky membership. CraftSky nevertheless treats the service as likely to be
accessed by children and applies the safeguards required by law.
