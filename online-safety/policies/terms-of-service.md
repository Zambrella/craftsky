## 9. Moderation and Account Standing

Depending on the facts, severity, context, intent, history, and risk, CraftSky may
apply a formal warning, visibility warning, visibility hide, visibility takedown,
strike, or suspension.

An account is suspended while at least three strikes remain active. A documented
severe violation may cause immediate suspension, which remains until CraftSky
restores the account or reverses the decision. Strikes ordinarily stop counting
after 12 calendar months unless reversed earlier. Enforcement remains active
while an appeal is pending.

Suspended members retain read access and can review moderation history, appeal,
manage private settings and subscriptions, make privacy requests, remove their
own content, sign out, and delete their CraftSky account. They cannot post or
interact publicly while suspended.

Moderation controls what CraftSky surfaces and which CraftSky functions an
account can use. It does not deactivate your DID or PDS account, remove you from
the wider AT Protocol network, or ordinarily delete your source PDS records.

We aim to apply these Terms consistently. Where reasonable and safe, we will tell
you about owner-visible action and how to appeal. We may withhold information
where disclosure would endanger someone, reveal another person's private data,
compromise security, prejudice an investigation, or break the law.

## 11. Blocking and Muting

A CraftSky block is a public AT Protocol record and restricts viewing and
interaction symmetrically in compliant CraftSky clients. Other AT Protocol
services may implement blocking differently. A mute is a private, one-way
CraftSky setting that changes what the muting user sees without notifying the
muted account.

Account deletion may become irreversible as soon as you complete the final
confirmation. CraftSky may retain narrowly limited information where law requires

Cancel paid subscription renewal through the billing provider before deleting
that CraftSky account. Account deletion does not cancel the provider's

has stopped and any pending payment or checkout has been resolved before
completing deletion. Complimentary access does not block deletion and ends when
the account is deleted. A subscription cannot be transferred to another CraftSky
