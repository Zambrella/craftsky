## 1. Respecting Rights

CraftSky respects copyright, trade marks, and other intellectual-property rights.
Members must post only material they have the right to share. Attribution does
not replace permission, although copyright exceptions may permit some uses
without permission.

## 2. Making a Notice

Email `moderation@craftsky.social` with `Copyright notice` or `Trade mark notice`
in the subject. A notice should include:

- your name and a safe way to contact you;
- whether you are the rights holder or authorized to act for them;
- a clear description of the protected work, trade mark, or other right;
- the CraftSky post, profile, event, account, or other location concerned;
- an explanation of why you believe the use infringes the right;
- any registration number or supporting document that is relevant;
- the territory in which the right applies;
- a good-faith statement that the information is accurate; and
- your physical or electronic signature.

Do not send identity documents, confidential contracts, or other sensitive
material unless CraftSky asks for information that is necessary to assess the
notice. If a notice is incomplete, CraftSky may ask for clarification before
deciding it.

## 3. Review and Action

CraftSky aims to acknowledge a notice within five UK business days. The time
needed for a decision depends on the complexity of the rights and evidence.

A notice is an allegation, not a finding. CraftSky may ask either party for more
information and will consider applicable exceptions, licences, ownership,
territory, and context. Depending on the evidence, CraftSky may take no action,
warn or hide material, take it out of CraftSky surfaces, restrict an account, or
apply another action permitted by the Terms and Community Guidelines.

Where reasonable and lawful, CraftSky tells the affected member what was alleged,
what action was taken, and how to respond. CraftSky may withhold personal,
confidential, unsafe, or legally protected information.

## 4. Responses and Restoration Requests

An affected member can respond or request restoration by emailing
`moderation@craftsky.social` within 30 calendar days of notice and including the
case reference. CraftSky may accept a late response where there is a good reason.
The response should explain why the member believes the material is authorized,
licensed, independently created, covered by an exception, outside the asserted
right, or otherwise non-infringing, and should include relevant evidence.

CraftSky will review the original notice, the response, and any further evidence.
It may uphold, change, or reverse its action. Restoration on CraftSky does not
decide ownership or prevent either party from seeking a court order or another
legal remedy.

## 5. Repeated or Abusive Conduct

Repeated infringement may lead to strikes or suspension, taking account of the
number, timing, seriousness, evidence, reversals, and surrounding circumstances.
CraftSky does not treat report volume alone as proof.

Do not knowingly make a materially false, malicious, or misleading notice or
response. CraftSky may warn or proportionately restrict a person who abuses this
process, while preserving access to legally required notices and complaints.

## 6. AT Protocol Limits

CraftSky moderation controls what CraftSky displays. It does not ordinarily
delete the source record from a member's PDS. Another AT Protocol service may
continue to display a public record under its own rules. A rights holder may also
need to contact the relevant PDS operator, another displaying service, or a court.

CraftSky's permanent account-deletion process is separate from this policy and is
described in the Terms and Privacy Policy.

## 7. Privacy and Legal Disclosure

CraftSky uses notice, response, evidence, identity, contact, decision, and
correspondence information to assess claims, protect rights, prevent abuse, and
meet legal duties. The [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md) explains retention,
recipients, rights, and legal holds.

Information may be shared where reasonably necessary to assess the notice, with
the parties, service providers, professional advisers, courts, regulators, law
enforcement, or another person where permitted or required by law.

## 8. Contact

Rights notices, responses, and restoration requests:
`moderation@craftsky.social`

General or accessibility questions: `support@craftsky.social`

Both inboxes are checked on UK business days and are not emergency-response
services. This procedure is available in English.
