## Purpose

This policy explains how people can report safety concerns, complain about
CraftSky's handling of illegal content, and appeal moderation decisions.

This draft is not yet operative. When it takes effect, signed-in CraftSky members
will be able to report posts, profiles, accounts, and business events in the app.
People who cannot sign in will be able to report or complain by email as described
below.

## Report Content or Conduct

People can report:

- suspected illegal content or criminal activity;
- child sexual exploitation, grooming, or child sexual abuse material;
- credible threats, terrorism, violence, or encouragement of serious self-harm;
- intimate-image abuse or cyberflashing;
- hate, harassment, stalking, doxxing, or block evasion;
- sexual exploitation, pornography, or graphic content;
- fraud, scams, phishing, impersonation, or counterfeit goods;
- spam or platform manipulation;
- copyright, trade mark, or other rights infringement;
- dangerous or materially false claims;
- generative-AI content prohibited by the Community Guidelines;
- off-topic content or another Community Guidelines violation; and
- a safety concern that does not fit a listed category.

A report is an allegation. It does not establish that the reported account broke
a rule. Multiple reports about one subject are grouped for review and report
volume does not determine guilt or the number of sanctions.

## Who Can Report

CraftSky accepts reports from:

- signed-in current CraftSky members;
- people directly affected by content even if they are not a CraftSky user;
- a parent, carer, or representative acting for an affected person; and
- organizations with a legitimate interest in the content where the law requires
  access to reporting.

The in-app route requires authentication. People who cannot sign in, affected
non-users, representatives, and qualifying organizations can email
`moderation@craftsky.social`. Pseudonymous reports are accepted where possible,
although a working contact method or identity evidence may be necessary for some
legal reports or to investigate the concern.

## What to Include

Provide the post, profile, event, or other location; select the closest reason;
and add only the context needed to understand the concern. Do not download or
redistribute suspected CSAM, intimate images, or other illegal material to make a
report.

CraftSky report details are private. CraftSky does not disclose reporter identity
or report text to the reported account unless disclosure is authorized by the
reporter, needed to protect someone, or otherwise permitted or required by law.
Relevant content and metadata may be preserved under restricted access where law
requires it.

## Urgent and Special Reports

### Immediate danger

If someone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services first. The
moderation inbox is checked on UK business days. CraftSky does not provide an
emergency service or promise immediate or out-of-hours monitoring.

### Child sexual exploitation and abuse

Do not attach or forward suspected child sexual abuse material. Report its
location. CraftSky will report detected and unreported CSEA content to the
National Crime Agency as required by section 66, except where content is covered
by a qualifying foreign-agency or group-undertaking reporting arrangement under
section 70.

### Intimate-image abuse

A depicted person or someone acting for them can make a qualifying report by
emailing `moderation@craftsky.social` with `Intimate image report` in the subject.
The email should identify the content without attaching or redistributing it,
provide a safe contact method, explain whether the sender is the depicted person
or their representative, and confirm in good faith that the report is true to the
sender's best knowledge and belief. Qualifying reports receive expedited
handling. A person who cannot safely provide all requested information should
send what they can and explain the concern.

CraftSky operates proportionate systems designed to take reported intimate
image content, and identified same or substantially similar content, down from
CraftSky as soon as reasonably practicable and no later than 48 hours after a
qualifying report. This duty does not apply where CraftSky considers that the
content is not statutory intimate-image content or that the reporter is neither
the depicted person nor acting on that person's behalf. Independent AT Protocol
copies may remain outside CraftSky's control.

## What Happens After a Report

CraftSky will:

1. acknowledge receipt and provide a reference;
2. triage urgency and specialist-handling needs;
3. preserve only necessary evidence under restricted access;
4. assess the content using available context and the applicable law, Terms, and
   Community Guidelines;
5. record a violation or no-action decision;
6. apply proportionate action;
7. tell the affected account about owner-visible action without exposing reporter
   identity or unsafe evidence; and
8. tell the reporter whether action was taken or no violation was found, without
   disclosing private sanctions or evidence; and
9. retain an auditable decision and any required legal records.

CraftSky aims to acknowledge reports, complaints, and appeals within five UK
business days. Final decision times depend on urgency, complexity, available
evidence, and legal duties. CraftSky does not promise a fixed final-response time,
but shorter statutory deadlines, including the intimate-image deadline above,
still apply.

## Complaints About CraftSky's Handling

Email `moderation@craftsky.social` to complain about:

- content a person considers illegal;
- CraftSky's response to an illegal-content report;
- a failure in CraftSky's reporting or complaints systems;
- failure to comply with applicable freedom-of-expression and privacy duties;
- removal or restriction of content because CraftSky considered it illegal;
- a warning, restriction, suspension, or ban based on content considered illegal;
- the use of proactive technology outside what CraftSky's Terms explain;
- content considered harmful to children;
- failure to comply with applicable child-safety duties;
- removal or restriction of a user's content because it was considered harmful
  to children;
- a warning, restriction, suspension, or ban based on content considered harmful
  to children;
- an age-assurance result that incorrectly assesses a user's age, if CraftSky
  introduces age assurance; and
- another failure to follow an applicable published policy.

The procedure is available in English by email and is intended to be clear and
usable by 16- and 17-year-olds. Contact `support@craftsky.social` if an
accommodation is needed. CraftSky will acknowledge, investigate, and communicate
outcomes. A separate data-protection complaint must be
acknowledged within the applicable statutory period; since 19 June 2026 that is
30 days under Data Protection Act 2018 section 164A.

A qualifying complaint relating to an intimate-image report must use the
expedited procedure required by Online Safety Act section 21(2A).

## Appeals by Affected Account Owners

Every owner-visible violation decision may be appealed. The moderation history
shows a case reference. Email `moderation@craftsky.social` within 30 calendar days
of notice and include that reference in the subject. CraftSky may accept a late
appeal where there is a good reason.

- Enforcement remains active while an appeal is pending.
- Email correspondence is untrusted and does not change enforcement by itself.
- A moderator confirms the appeal against the case before it becomes pending.
- CraftSky does not disclose private case information merely because a sender
  knows a case reference.
- One appeal lifecycle is recorded per case. Further correspondence may still be
  reviewed and any later effect change must be audited.
- The outcome is recorded as upheld or changed. Reversed effects stop applying;
  effects that were not reversed remain active.
- A different reviewer decides the appeal wherever practicable. If only one
  moderator is available, the original moderator may reconsider the decision and
  must record that they conducted both reviews.

## Misuse

Do not submit knowingly false, malicious, automated, or retaliatory reports or
use reporting to harass another person. CraftSky may restrict abuse of reporting,
normally after a warning, but preserves access to urgent and legally required
reports. A person can appeal a reporting restriction by emailing
`moderation@craftsky.social`.

## External Routes

People may also:

- [raise an online-safety concern with Ofcom](https://www.ofcom.org.uk/make-a-complaint), although Ofcom does not decide individual moderation disputes;
- [make a data-protection complaint to the ICO](https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/);
- contact local law enforcement or emergency services where appropriate;
- use any applicable court, app-store, or other legal remedy; or
- use the separate [Copyright and Trade Mark Policy](copyright-and-trade-marks.md).
