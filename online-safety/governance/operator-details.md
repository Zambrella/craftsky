The [ICO's DPO guidance](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/accountability-and-governance/guide-to-accountability-and-governance/data-protection-officers/)
was checked on 9 November 2026. Mandatory appointment depends on public-authority
status, core large-scale regular/systematic monitoring, or core large-scale
processing of special-category or criminal-offence data. Company headcount alone
does not decide the requirement. A formal DPO must be independent and free of
conflicting duties; a founder who decides processing purposes and means presents
a conflict that must be addressed if a DPO is required. An external DPO is an
available option.
