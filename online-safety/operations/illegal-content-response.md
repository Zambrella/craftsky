## Start here

Check the moderation console and `moderation@craftsky.social` for safety reports,
and `support@craftsky.social` for privacy. Check both inboxes on UK business days
and act on urgent concerns as soon as you become aware. Douglas owns triage,
decisions and follow-up. Prioritise by immediacy, severity, continued exposure
and whether a child or another vulnerable person is at risk. CraftSky has one
operator and no confirmed backup or 24-hour cover.

Use the existing case or specialist workflow. For email, use **External intake →
Record external intake** with the original receipt time, a stable message
reference (such as Message-ID), the subject's AT URI if available, the type and
urgency. Link the intake to the existing or new case as appropriate. Keep the
email in the mailbox and log later emails on the same intake. Keep harmful
material, private records and credentials out of ordinary notes, chat and logs.
The [moderation console reference](../../docs/operations/moderation-reference.md)
has the detailed controls.

## Immediate danger

1. **Call 999 for a UK emergency.** Give the minimum reliable facts about who
   is at risk, what happened, when and where. If the location is unknown, say so
   when seeking UK police assistance. For a known overseas location use the
   official emergency route for that location. Seek help before completing
   paperwork. [UK police contact guidance](https://www.gov.uk/contact-police)
2. Restrict harmful content or contact in CraftSky where appropriate. Use
   **Specialist workflows → Start credible threat review** to record the action,
   agency reference and next step. If CSEA is also detected, follow the NCA route
   below. Apply the disclosure steps for any private data you need to share.

## Child sexual exploitation or abuse

1. Minimise viewing and restrict access in CraftSky promptly. If a child is in
   immediate danger, follow the emergency steps above. Preserve required
   material only in authorised restricted systems. Keep it out of ordinary
   downloads, screenshots, forwarded emails and moderation notes.
2. Assess the facts against the CSEA reporting duty. Check for a known
   applicable prior report and record the reason if it is a duplicate or
   does not meet the reporting threshold. Use **Safety incidents** if a
   record exists. Otherwise keep the chronology, decision and receipt in
   a restricted operator record. Make any required referral even if there
   is no moderation case.
   [Ofcom CSEA guidance](https://www.ofcom.org.uk/online-safety/illegal-and-harmful-content/duty-to-report-child-sexual-exploitation-and-abuse-csea-content-know-the-rules-and-how-to-comply)
3. Report detected, unreported CSEA manually using the
   [NCA portal](https://www.uk-child-sexual-abuse-industry-reporting.gov.uk/home).
   **Priority 1:** immediate threat to a child's life or immediate risk of
   serious harm, report **immediately**. **Priority 2:** possible serious harm
   soon or urgent safeguarding, report **as soon as reasonably practicable**.
   **Priority 3:** other reportable cases, report **without undue delay**.
   Send the required information available at the time and submit a linked
   supplement as soon as missing information becomes available.
4. Record the actual receipt and reference in the restricted record. Respond to
   an NCA information request as soon as reasonably practicable and within
   **7 days**. Keep the report reference for **5 years from issue**. Keep the
   content, submitted information, judgment material and applicable associated
   user data for **1 year from submission** under regulation 8. The two-week
   lookback for associated data runs back from the relevant upload, creation,
   sharing or receipt, as applicable. Set a retention review and record any
   separately applicable hold. [CSEA Reporting Regulations, regs. 6–8 and schedules](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/268/made/data.html)

**If the portal is down:** for Priority 1 use the NCA contingency form, send it
to the specified NCA mailbox and follow up on the number in the form. Send the
full report via the portal when restored. Never attach illegal content to the
contingency email. For urgent Priority 2 or uncertainty about out-of-hours
phone follow-up, follow current NCA instructions and seek prompt clarification
via an official NCA contact. The supplied manual guide and standalone
contingency form differ on Priority 2. [Portal onboarding record](#portal-onboarding-and-reporting-exercise--26-november-2026)

**If evidence storage is unavailable:** continue the protective action and
contact the NCA promptly for secure handling instructions if the failure
affects reporting or retention. Record the issue in the restricted incident
and use the [restricted evidence runbook](../../docs/operations/restricted-evidence-storage.md).

## Intimate images shared without consent

1. Open **Intimate image review**, linked to the intake where available. Use
   the original receipt time, including when discovered later. CraftSky's
   **24-hour internal target** and the **48-hour statutory limit** for qualifying
   reports both run from receipt. Act sooner whenever practicable. If the form
   is unavailable, record the receipt and deadline securely and act anyway.
2. Capture the content reference, contact details and declarations. The
   reporter should declare that the content is intimate-image content, that
   they are depicted or act on behalf of the person depicted, and that the
   report is in good faith and true to the best of their knowledge and belief.
   Assess the content and standing. If the statutory route does not apply,
   record why and still assess other safety or policy routes.
3. Take qualifying content down from CraftSky as soon as reasonably
   practicable and by 48 hours. Use proportionate searches for the same or
   substantially the same content and take identified copies down by that
   deadline. Check actual visibility and record searches, limits and outcome.
   Reply safely and expedite complaints about the handling.
   [Online Safety Act, section 10](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2023/50/section/10) · [Section 20A](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2023/50/section/20A)

## Routine moderation, complaints and appeals

- **Report.** Review the correct subject, available context and applicable
  rule. Decide **No action** or apply a proportionate consequence supported by
  the facts and the console. For suspected illegal content, record the legal
  reasoning privately and act swiftly when the assessment supports removal.
  CraftSky moderation changes CraftSky visibility and account access. Leave
  source PDS records intact. Verify the effect and record the reason.
- **Reply.** Aim to acknowledge reports, complaints and appeals within
  **5 UK business days** with a reference, and decide routine reports within
  **10 UK business days** from receipt. Urgent and statutory deadlines take
  priority. Give a safe outcome where there is a contact route, honour any
  recorded communication preference, and protect reporter identity and private
  case details.
- **Handling complaint or appeal.** Link it to the case where applicable.
  Verify the affected owner before sharing private case details. The normal
  appeal window is **30 calendar days from notice**. Consider good reasons for
  late appeals. Investigate what was missed or mishandled. If you made the
  original decision, record your reconsideration. Keep existing effects during
  an appeal and use appeal resolution to reverse any selected effects if the
  decision changes. Confirm the resulting standing and visibility and give a
  safe explanation. [Reporting, Complaints and Appeals Policy](../policies/reporting-complaints-and-appeals.md)
- **Suspected under-16 account.** Review the actual eligibility evidence
  through the account workspace. Use the age restriction or restoration
  action if justified, record the reason, and give the user a way to
  challenge it. Avoid collecting full dates of birth or identity documents
  by default.
- **Bluesky DMs.** Use the Bluesky report route for messages or conversations.
  Keep transcripts out of CraftSky intake. Follow the emergency or legal
  route where applicable if you learn of a safeguarding concern.
- **Copyright or trade marks.** Use the dedicated notice, response and
  restoration procedure. [Copyright and Trade Mark Policy](../policies/copyright-and-trade-marks.md)

## Authority requests

1. Use **Specialist workflows → Record authority request**. Verify the request
   and the requester through independently obtained official contact details.
   Record whether it is compulsory or voluntary and the specific legal
   authority where applicable. If unverified or unclear, hold disclosure and
   record refusal or the clarification needed.
2. Before sharing, record the lawful basis, necessity and smallest justified
   scope. Check any additional sensitive-data and overseas-transfer conditions.
   Share only necessary data CraftSky controls, with the verified recipient
   over a secure channel. Keep credentials and unrelated records out of it.
3. Record the decision, fields actually shared, date and safe transfer reference
   in the restricted workflow. The console records the decision and transfer
   details. Send any approved disclosure separately. For a justified
   preservation hold, record its basis, scope and review or release date.
   [ICO guidance on sharing with law enforcement](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/data-sharing/sharing-personal-data-with-law-enforcement-authorities/)

## Personal data breach

1. Contain the exposure, preserve relevant logs securely and contact the affected
   provider if needed. Record when CraftSky became aware, what data and people
   are affected, the likely harm and the steps taken.
2. If the breach is likely to pose a risk to people's rights or freedoms,
   notify the ICO without undue delay and within **72 hours of awareness**
   where feasible. Send what is known and follow up as facts become available.
   If the risk is high, also inform affected people without undue delay.
3. Record the decision and rationale even if no notification is required.
   Track containment and fixes to completion.
   [ICO personal data breach guide](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/report-a-breach/personal-data-breach/personal-data-breaches-a-guide/)

## Privacy requests and complaints

- **Rights request.** Record receipt, requested action and due date. Check
  identity or representative authority proportionately. Search the systems
  and providers CraftSky controls, protect other people's data, and explain
  any independent PDS or provider limits. Respond securely and record the
  outcome. Subject access is generally due within **one month**. Check the
  applicable rules for other rights. [ICO subject access guide](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/subject-access-requests/a-guide-to-subject-access/)
- **Privacy complaint.** Aim to acknowledge within **5 UK business days** and
  do so within **30 days**. Investigate promptly, keep the person informed and
  explain the outcome and how to contact the ICO. Track any underlying rights
  request on its own deadline.
  [ICO complaints guidance](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/advice-for-small-organisations/what-s-the-right-way-to-handle-a-data-protection-complaint/)

## Close the loop

In the same case or restricted workflow, leave the original receipt or
detection time, decision and reason, action taken, external reference if any,
and the next action and due time. Keep unfinished reporting, follow-up and
retention visible even if moderation is complete. After a serious incident or
control failure, update the relevant risk assessment with what was learned.
