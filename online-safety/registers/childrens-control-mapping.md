## Basis and boundaries

Read the [issued PCU Code](https://www.ofcom.org.uk/siteassets/resources/documents/consultations/category-1-10-weeks/statement-protecting-children-from-harms-online/upcoming-codes-of-practice/issued-protection-of-children-code-of-practice-for-user-to-user-services.pdf?v=427439)
in force 2 November 2026, its application clauses and definitions, together with
the currently linked children's risk/content guidance. This register covers all
52 numbered application clauses and records unused sections. It is an initial
applicability/evidence review, not a completed Code-compliance declaration.

The 14 proposed Medium content ratings are independent findings awaiting owner
review; they are not copied from the illegal-content assessment. Actual risk
and service facts govern applicability, not the act of signing a document.
The conditional mapping must be reconciled with final findings and effective
published policy. Previously approved High grooming/overall CSEA judgments stay
in the separate illegal-content assessment; neither directly sets PCU content
ratings nor triggers PCU J1/J2 by itself.
