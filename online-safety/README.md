# CraftSky online safety

This directory contains the source files for CraftSky's public policies.
Internal incident procedures, assessments and approval records are managed
separately from this index. Use the
[release runbook](../docs/operations/releases.md#private-beta-and-full-public-launch)
for the component checks and deployment steps.

## Keeping records current

- Keep internal records out of public builds. Publish only the approved policy
  routes using the allowlist and retain superseded policy versions.
- Record the owner, scope, status, review date and evidence for each assessment.
  For approvals, record who decided, when, what evidence they considered, the
  safeguards chosen, applicable Ofcom measures or alternatives, and accepted
  residual risks. Track unresolved work in the internal records.
- Review at least annually and when law, guidance, risks or safeguards change
  or a serious incident occurs. Update affected assessments before significant
  service changes, and review compliance promptly after implementation.
